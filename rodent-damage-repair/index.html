What rodents actually damage.

Beyond the obvious mess of droppings and nesting, rodents cause real structural wear: chewed attic insulation that loses its effectiveness, gnawed soffit panels and fascia boards, and in more serious cases, chewed electrical wiring that is a genuine fire risk, not just a cosmetic problem.

Drywall damage usually comes from water intrusion through a gap the rodents originally used, or from a wall cavity that was opened to remove a dead animal, rather than direct gnawing through the wall itself.

What gets assessed first.

  • Attic insulation condition and R-value loss from nesting and tunneling.
  • Soffit, fascia and roof vent condition at every former entry point.
  • Visible wiring damage, documented for an electrician to address.
  • Any drywall or ceiling staining from related water intrusion.
Repair without sealing just gives the next rodent something new to chew.

Repair happens after sealing, not before.

Fixing chewed soffits or replacing insulation before the entry points are actually closed means repeating the repair in a few months. Our repair work is scheduled after exclusion and entry point sealing so the fix has a real chance to hold.

Insulation replacement.

Attic insulation that has been tunneled through, flattened or soiled with droppings loses much of its insulating value, which matters in a Florida attic that already runs hot. Where replacement makes sense, old material is removed and bagged following the same safe-handling approach used for cleanup.

What we do not handle directly.

Electrical wiring repair itself is done by a licensed electrician, not by us; we document the damage clearly so that repair, or an insurance claim, has what it needs. The same applies to any structural repair beyond soffit, fascia and insulation work.

After repair.

Confirming the fix holds.

  • Sealed entry points checked again on the follow-up visit.
  • New insulation checked for disturbance or new nesting.
  • Any documented wiring damage followed up with your electrician.

Before we arrive

  • Keep children and pets away from any area with droppings, a nest, or a suspected entry point.
  • Do not sweep or vacuum dry droppings; that can spread contamination into the air.
  • Do not set out your own loose bait or poison before the visit.
  • Note when and where you have seen or heard activity so the technician can start there.

Questions about rodent damage repair

Do you repair chewed electrical wiring?

No, that work goes to a licensed electrician, but we document exactly what we find so that repair or an insurance claim has clear information to work from.

Should insulation always be replaced after a rodent problem?

Not always; it depends on how much was disturbed or contaminated. We assess it honestly rather than recommending a full replacement by default.

Can repair happen in the same visit as sealing?

Often yes for smaller soffit or fascia repairs, though larger insulation replacement is sometimes scheduled as a separate visit.
