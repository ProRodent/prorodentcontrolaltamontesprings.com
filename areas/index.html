Coverage overview

We only list a town on this page if it checked out two ways: it appears in our real coverage data, and its distance from Altamonte Springs was independently verified against that same ZIP code. A couple of these towns are unincorporated communities rather than their own cities, and we say so plainly rather than describing them as something they are not.

One of these towns, Orlando, is also a far larger city than any other on this list. We only cover the ZIP codes within a reasonable distance of our base, not the whole metro area, and we explain that honestly on that town's own page rather than implying city-wide coverage we cannot really deliver on schedule.
