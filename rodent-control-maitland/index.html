Call about Maitland

One of the area's oldest incorporated towns.

Maitland was first incorporated in 1885 and reorganized as a city in 1959, and the city includes older lakeside neighborhoods around Lake Maitland and Lake Sybelia alongside newer infill construction. For a rodent call the age of the individual house matters more than the city's age: an older roof tends to have original vents and soffits to check, while newer infill has more utility penetrations, so tell us the year built when you call.

The lakes that give Maitland its name and layout, there are several within the city limits, create the same kind of tree-and-water edge that roof rats favor, which is the main pattern our technicians look for on a Maitland call.

What inspections here typically find.

  • Roof rat activity along lakefront tree canopy near Lake Maitland and Lake Sybelia.
  • Older roof vent styles on homes dating to the city's earlier development.
  • Garage and storage-area mouse activity on both older and newer homes.

Crossing into Orange County.

Maitland sits just across the Seminole-Orange County line from our Altamonte Springs base, a short enough distance that it does not change scheduling or pricing, only the jurisdiction noted for our own records.

The sealing and trapping approach is identical to any other address we cover: inspection first, treatment matched to what is actually found, and a follow-up to confirm it held.

Booking a Maitland visit.

Call the same line used for Altamonte Springs itself and mention Maitland and your nearest lake or cross street. Same-day visits are offered where the schedule allows.

On a lakefront lot the outside check comes first: rub marks along limbs that reach the roof, droppings along fence tops, and gaps where the roof meets the wall. Those signs show which route the animals use before anyone goes into the attic.

If the address is a business, office or institutional building rather than a home, tell us on the call. Those buildings get the same roofline and utility-penetration check, plus a look at dumpster areas and rear doors, and the visit can be planned around operating hours.

Before we arrive in Maitland.

  • Mention whether the home is near Lake Maitland, Lake Sybelia, or another lake in town.
  • Note any tree limbs touching the roofline.
  • Keep stored boxes away from exterior walls in the garage if activity has been seen there.

Before we arrive

  • Keep children and pets clear of any spot with droppings or a likely entry point.
  • Avoid dry-sweeping droppings; dampen the area with disinfectant first, or leave it for the technician.
  • Jot down when and where you noticed activity, even roughly.

Tell us on the call

  • Your address and the closest cross street.
  • What you have noticed and roughly where.
  • Whether the home has children or pets.
