Why cars are an easy target.

A parked car is warm, dry and quiet, which makes the engine bay and cabin air intake an attractive nesting spot for mice and roof rats, especially a vehicle that sits for days at a time in a garage, driveway or long-term parking area.

The soy-based insulation used on a lot of modern wiring harnesses is also a food source for rodents, which is part of why chewed wiring has become a common and expensive outcome of a vehicle infestation that goes unnoticed.

Where activity shows up.

  • Nesting material packed around the air filter housing or cabin air intake.
  • Droppings on the engine shield or inside the engine bay.
  • Chewed wiring insulation, often near the harness connectors.
  • A persistent odor from the HVAC vents when the car runs.
Chewed wiring in a car is not just annoying, it can be genuinely dangerous.

What a visit covers.

A technician checks the engine bay, cabin air intake and surrounding area for nesting material, droppings and chew marks, and removes what is found. We do not do mechanical wiring repair ourselves, but we document the damage clearly for a mechanic or insurance claim, and we address the nesting and entry points so the problem does not just come back.

Preventing repeat activity.

Reducing the risk going forward.

  • Running the engine regularly breaks up a nest before it is established.
  • Popping the hood periodically on a rarely-driven car lets you check early.
  • Peppermint-based repellents have mixed results and are not a substitute for removal.

Before we arrive

  • Keep children and pets away from any area with droppings, a nest, or a suspected entry point.
  • Do not sweep or vacuum dry droppings; that can spread contamination into the air.
  • Do not set out your own loose bait or poison before the visit.
  • Note when and where you have seen or heard activity so the technician can start there.

Questions about vehicle and car rodent removal

Can you repair the chewed wiring?

We handle removal of the nesting and animals and document the damage, but mechanical wiring repair is done by a mechanic; we are glad to describe exactly what was found for that repair or an insurance claim.

Will the smell from the vents go away?

Once the nesting material and any droppings are removed from the air intake area, the odor usually clears within a few drives as the HVAC system cycles fresh air.

Does this happen more with electric or hybrid vehicles?

It can, since those vehicles may sit quieter and warmer for longer stretches, giving rodents more undisturbed time to nest.
