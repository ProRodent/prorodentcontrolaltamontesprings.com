Near Altamonte Mall.

Altamonte Mall has anchored this part of the city since 1974. The restaurants, loading docks and parking structures around it draw rodent activity that can spill over into the nearby residential streets of Academy Cove, especially where dumpster areas sit close to a property line.

Academy Cove sits close enough to Altamonte Mall that the commercial activity around the mall, deliveries, dumpsters, restaurant kitchens, can influence what residential properties nearby deal with, on top of the usual causes like tree cover and aging entry points.

The area has a mix of apartments and single-family homes, which means the inspection approach varies by property: a single-family house gets the usual roofline-to-foundation check, while an apartment building gets the same shared-wall and utility-chase attention a condo would.

What we check in this area.

  • Dumpster and loading areas close to residential property lines.
  • Shared walls and utility chases in apartment buildings.
  • Established tree and shrub cover around older single-family lots.

A neighborhood where timing matters.

Because commercial activity near the mall can push rodents toward residential streets especially during cooler months when shelter options narrow, acting on early signs here, a single dropping, a single scratching sound, tends to pay off more than waiting to see if it gets worse.

Apartment and single-family coverage.

If you live in an apartment here and suspect a neighboring unit has an active problem, mention that when you call; shared-wall activity often needs coordination with a property manager even when only one unit called in the first place.

Because Academy Cove sits close enough to Altamonte Mall's loading and dumpster areas that commercial activity can influence nearby residential calls, we sometimes ask whether neighbors have reported similar activity recently, since that can mean the source is shared rather than isolated to one property.

Building age changes the plan: an older single-family block usually points to roof vents, soffits and garage seals, while an apartment complex points to shared walls, utility chases and dumpster enclosures. Tell us which you have when you call.

Before we arrive

  • Keep children and pets away from any area with droppings, a nest, or a suspected entry point.
  • Do not sweep or vacuum dry droppings; that can spread contamination into the air.
  • Do not set out your own loose bait or poison before the visit.
  • Note when and where you have seen or heard activity so the technician can start there.

Questions about Academy Cove

Does the mall cause the rodent problem here?

Commercial activity nearby, dumpsters and deliveries in particular, can push rodents toward residential streets, but every property still gets its own inspection rather than an assumption based on proximity alone.

Do apartment buildings get the same service as houses?

Yes, with extra attention to shared walls and utility chases that a single-family home does not have.

Could my neighbor's property be the real source of the problem?

It is possible, especially this close to commercial activity near the mall; mentioning any activity you know of at neighboring properties helps us understand the fuller picture, even though treatment is still scoped to your own property.
