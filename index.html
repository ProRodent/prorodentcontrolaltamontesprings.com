Signs you have rodents

Check your home for these. Two or more usually means an active problem.

  • Droppings

    Dark pellets along baseboards, in drawers, under sinks or in the attic.

  • Gnaw marks

    Fresh gnawing leaves pale, rough wood on soffits, wiring and stored boxes.

  • Noises at night

    Scratching or scurrying in the attic, walls or ceiling after dark, more common once the weather cools.

  • Nesting material

    Shredded insulation, cardboard or palm fiber packed into hidden corners.

  • Rub marks

    Greasy smears along soffit edges, fence tops and pipes on regular routes.

  • Odors

    A musty, ammonia-like smell in closed rooms or near interior walls.

Recognize two or more?

Rodent control for Central Florida homes

Altamonte Springs sits in Seminole County where mild winters mean rodent activity rarely slows down the way it does further north. Roof rats in particular do well in Central Florida's tree canopy and warm, humid climate, moving from palms and oaks onto rooflines and into attics year round.

That year-round pressure is the main reason a one-time treatment rarely holds on its own. Mouse control and rat control both start with the same question: where is the animal actually getting in. We trace the travel routes first, then treat and seal, instead of just setting traps and hoping.

Older homes often have different gaps than newer construction, such as original roof vents and soffit returns versus utility penetrations and garage door seals. Tell us the age of the house when you call and we plan the visit around what that house style usually needs.

Questions about rodent control

How can I tell if I have mice or rats?

Look for droppings, gnaw marks, greasy rub marks and night-time scratching in the attic or walls. Rat droppings are noticeably larger than mouse droppings, and roof rats are more often heard overhead than underfoot.

How do rodents get into homes in Altamonte Springs?

Mice can pass through gaps of about a quarter inch and rats through roughly half an inch, so roof vents, soffit returns, plumbing chases and garage door gaps are the common entry points we find.

Will traps alone solve the problem?

Trapping removes the animals already inside, but sealing the entry points is what stops new ones from arriving once the warm season brings activity back up.

Is treatment safe around children and pets?

Tell the technician about every child and pet in the home, and we explain what was used and exactly where before we leave.

How quickly can someone come out?

Our line is answered around the clock and same-day visits are offered where the schedule allows.

What happens after the first visit?

We check the traps, confirm the seals are holding and advise on any cleanup the household needs to do.
