Call the line.

Describe what you are seeing or hearing, give your street and nearest cross street, and mention whether it is inside Altamonte Springs or one of the nearby towns we cover. Scheduling starts from there.

If you are not sure whether what you are dealing with is a mouse, a rat, or something else entirely, describe it anyway. A scratching sound in the attic at night, droppings along a baseboard, a chewed corner on a stored box, each points toward a different likely culprit. The person answering the line can usually narrow it down well enough to schedule the right kind of visit before a technician ever sees the property in person.

Email.

What to have ready when you call.

  • Your address and the nearest cross street, especially if you are in one of the nearby towns rather than Altamonte Springs itself.
  • A rough sense of how long you have noticed activity, even if it is only a guess.
  • Whether children or pets are in the home, since that can affect what materials get used and where.
  • Whether the issue is inside the house, in the garage, in the attic, or outdoors, if you can tell.

None of this needs to be exact. The technician's own inspection is what actually determines the plan; the phone call is mainly there to get someone out to the right address with the right basic information in hand.
