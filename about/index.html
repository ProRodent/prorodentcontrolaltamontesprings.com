How a visit is scoped.

Every job starts with an inspection, not a quote over the phone. A technician checks the attic, garage, soffits and exterior before recommending anything, because the right plan depends on where the animal is actually getting in, not just whether the house has mice or rats.

What we do not do.

We do not sell service based on a guess, and we do not leave a house with traps set and no plan to seal the entry points. We also do not use any rodenticide or exclusion material without walking the homeowner through where it was used.

Coverage and hours.

The inspection line is answered 24 hours a day, with same-day visits offered where the schedule allows. Coverage runs from Altamonte Springs's own ZIP 32701 out to the nearby Seminole and Orange County towns on our areas page.

Why inspection-first matters in this climate.

Central Florida's mild winters mean rodent activity rarely slows down the way it would further north. Roof rats in particular do well in the tree canopy common around Altamonte Springs, and a treatment plan built without a real inspection tends to miss the actual entry point, which means the same call repeats itself in a few weeks.

That is why every service on this site, from mouse and rat control through exclusion, cleanup and repair, starts from the same inspection rather than a generic package sold over the phone.

How the twelve services fit together.

Most jobs touch more than one service page before they are done. A typical rat job might move from inspection into trapping and removal, then into exclusion and sealing, and sometimes into attic cleanup or damage repair if the activity ran long enough to leave real contamination or chewed material behind.

  • An inspection decides the real scope, not a guess over the phone.
  • Trapping and removal clear what is already inside.
  • Exclusion and sealing stop the next animal from replacing it.
  • Cleanup and repair address whatever was left behind.

Who this line serves.

We work with homeowners across Altamonte Springs and the nearby towns on our areas page, and with businesses, shops, restaurants and offices, that need documented, scheduled pest management rather than a one-time emergency visit. Both get the same inspection-first approach, scaled to what the property actually needs.

How scheduling actually works.

When you call, the person answering is gathering the same basic information every time: your address, what you have seen or heard, and whether children or pets are in the home. That call is what gets a technician assigned, not a quote decided sight unseen. Same-day visits are offered whenever the day's schedule allows it, and if it does not, we will tell you honestly rather than promise a window we cannot keep.

Every inspection results in a written explanation of what was found and what is recommended, not just a verbal summary at the door. That gives you something to reference later, whether you are deciding between options or simply want a record of what was done and why.
